What is AllergenGo?

When a person with an allergy visits your restaurant, they often need to speak with a chef or browse a binder of ingredients. This method is outdated, prone to error, and potentially dangerous. Allergies can be life-threatening — and have resulted in fatalities in the past.

With AllergenGo, you enter your dishes and their allergens via your admin dashboard. The system then generates a unique QR code for diners to scan. After scanning, users enter their allergens, and the app instantly filters the menu to show only safe options.

This shifts responsibility to the customer in a safe and empowering way. Rather than relying on staff explanations, diners with allergies can view a complete menu, clearly marked with allergens, and feel confident in their choices.
